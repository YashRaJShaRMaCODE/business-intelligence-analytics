07 / METHOD & LIMITS

Transparent by design.

The app profiles the CSV bundled in this repository at startup. The source snapshot contains {{ "{:,}".format(profile.count) }} movie and television records; it is not a live service catalog, availability feed, or endorsement by Netflix or another platform.

Completeness is the share of non-empty cells across {{ profile.columns }} source fields. Genre tags are parsed as lists and normalized to lowercase for the chart and export. The download omits rows without a title, normalizes genre separators, and keeps other missing values blank. Possible duplicates are flagged for review, never silently deleted.
